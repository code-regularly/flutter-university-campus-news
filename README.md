## University Campus News - A Flutter & Dart App!

Let us a take a look at the idea behind the app along with the use cases and features that it offer.

### Concept & Overview

This app is a cross-platform, non-native, mobile-first Flutter- and Dart-based application built with the aim of improving communication throughout the university campuses by squeezing the communication gap among students, management and administration.

### Learning Curve & Development Timeline (2021)

I developed this app in the Summer break of 2021 as a rising final-year computer science and software engineering student, so that I could exercise my recently-acquired Flutter and Dart skills. The project spanned over 4 weeks (June - July, 2021), where, at first, I learnt the basics of Dart programming language and Flutter framework. Afterwards, I put all my learning into this one holistic project to create a real, tangible product.

### Features & Use Cases

As the app focuses on decreasing the communication gap across the campuses, it offers the following features as mentioned below. Remember, each feature gets its own place in a separate sequence of screens, which is setup inside the bottom tab navigator on the home page of the application. In this app you can:

- Share exciting university news, like the advent of the Technology Innovation Labs at the university or the appointment of new VC
- Share upcoming event details, like the election events or student society events.
- Make announcements for students and staff, like the new smoking policy on campus.
- Initiate hashtags, like #OpenUniversity, and react to them by agreeing or disagreeing with the author.
- One can add new news, events, announcements and hashtags after logging in using their university ID and password.
- The app also allows you to take a look at your profile as well as get information about the developer of the app (me :) through a drawer navigator, implemented in the home.
- You can also choose to log out of the app at any time by clicking the log out button in the drawer.

### Limitations & Simplifications

The current implementation of the app focuses only on bringing smooth and reliable communication experience across the university campus, but extensions can be made to make the app more generic by bringing other universities on-board as well.

### Future Extension Plans

As of now, no extension plans have been made. In case the idea catches on and there really is a need for one such app, it will be extended and a fully functional back-end will be added as well. This is only a front-end project, focused on Flutter.
